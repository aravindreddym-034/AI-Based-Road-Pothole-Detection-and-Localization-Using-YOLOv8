4. WHAT NOT TO DO (AVOID WASTING TIME)
================================================================================

1. STOP training models on your CPU:
   - Your baseline 50-epoch run took 21 hours and 9 minutes. If retraining or
     testing larger models, use free GPU cloud environments (Google Colab / Kaggle).

2. DO NOT train heavier architectures (YOLOv8l or YOLOv8x):
   - Excessive parameters (43M+) for a single-class 1,200-image dataset will
     overfit, run slowly on video, and contradict the edge-localization goal.

3. DO NOT over-engineer backend infrastructure:
   - Avoid Docker, Kubernetes, AWS microservices, or complex databases for an IDP.
   - A clean Streamlit application with local SQLite/CSV logging is simpler,
     more reliable during live vivas, and easier for recruiters to run.

4. DO NOT manually re-annotate the dataset:
   - 1,243 annotated images are sufficient for an academic capstone. Spending
     days tweaking bounding boxes provides minimal performance returns.
